**Challenge.** A captive technology centre faced repeated transfer-pricing adjustments on its cost-plus margin, creating uncertainty and litigation cost.

**Approach.** We prepared the APA application, benchmarked the margin, engaged with the APA authority, and negotiated a defensible arm's-length range with a rollback.

**Outcome.** A unilateral APA was concluded in about 18 months, giving the group multi-year certainty and ending repetitive disputes.
